What peadf does

Edits the text that’s already there

Many online PDF editors put a new text box over the old words. peadf changes the existing text instead: click a line and retype it, and the embedded font, size and colour are kept while the paragraph re-wraps around your change. Find and replace works across every page, so you can edit a PDF without losing its formatting or fix a typo in a PDF in seconds.

Forms, signatures and real redaction

Type into real form fields, tick boxes and choose from dropdowns, then flatten the form or keep it editable. Draw, type or upload a signature, or send a PDF to someone else to sign in their browser. When you redact a PDF, the content under the box is destroyed rather than hidden, so it can’t be copied back out.

Scans, pages and file size

peadf can read the text on a scan so you can edit a scanned PDF, and it does the recognition on your device. It also merges, splits, rotates, crops and reorders pages, adds page numbers or a watermark, compresses large files, and adds or removes a password.

Share a PDF as a link and QR code

Upload a PDF and peadf hosts it on a clean link that anyone can open in their browser, with no download, app or sign-up. Every file comes with a high-resolution QR code in PNG or SVG for menus, posters and packaging. Replace the file later and every link and code points to the new version, and you can see how many times it was opened, when and where.

Questions people ask about peadf

Can I edit the existing text in a PDF?

Yes. Click a line and retype it. peadf keeps the original embedded font, size and colour, and the paragraph re-wraps as you type.

Are my files uploaded when I edit?

No. Editing, form filling, redaction and OCR all run in your browser. A file only leaves your device if you choose to share it as a link.

Can I update a shared PDF without changing the link?

Yes. Replace the file and the same link and QR code open the new version, so printed codes and old messages still work.

Is peadf free?

The editor and the PDF tools are free to use in the browser, with no watermark and no sign-up to start. Downloading an edited PDF needs an account, and PDF hosting has a free plan.
